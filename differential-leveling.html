Field Terminology

Key Vocabulary

A quick refresher. Hover or tap a card to reveal its definition.

Benchmark (BM)

A point with a known elevation, used as your starting reference.

Backsight (BS)

The first rod reading, taken looking back at a point of known elevation.

Foresight (FS)

The final rod reading, taken looking ahead before the instrument moves.

Turning Point (TP)

A stable point used to carry elevation from one instrument setup to the next.

Height of Instrument (HI)

The elevation of the instrument's line of sight above your datum.

Elevation

The vertical position of a point relative to a reference elevation.

Digital Field Notebook

A technology-based field record used to collect, organize, and communicate survey information.

Survey Workflow

The process of collecting, documenting, reviewing, and presenting professional survey data.
